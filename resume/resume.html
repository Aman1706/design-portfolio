Design Project

Buddy Up — AI learning companion app for teens (iOS & Android)

2026
  • Designed an app where teens get an AI-generated weekly plan with daily goals, a personalised chat buddy (“Bud”) and topic communities, taking it from research to a clickable iOS and Android prototype.
  • Surveyed 14 students, interviewed teens with a team of four, and reviewed 15 learning, habit and community apps, including Duolingo, Finch and Khan Academy. None combined a personal plan, a companion and a community.
  • Turned the findings into a persona, empathy map and journey map, then a card sort of 53 features into 9 groups that shaped the information architecture, 12 task flows and 46 wireframe screens. Cut v1 to its core: points and streaks instead of a dashboard, search only inside Community, and Bud in place of a help centre.
  • Designed native iOS (HIG) and Android (Material) UI with documented component anatomy and a designed screen for every state, from loading to error.
  • Turned the Figma designs and prototype into a working coded iOS prototype with Claude, covering onboarding, plan setup, daily goals, quizzes, chat with Bud and community.
  • Tested the coded prototype with 3 users on 5 tasks, with 100% task completion. The feedback set the next steps: bringing reminders back and showing plan length up front.
